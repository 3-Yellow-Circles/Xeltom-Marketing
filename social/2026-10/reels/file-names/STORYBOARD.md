## Video direction

- palette system: frame.md roles. Frames 1–2 sit on deep navy (text #142038 used as ground) with white file rows and teal (primary #19b59b) only for the caret and highlight; frames 3–4 sit on the light canvas (bg #ffffff with soft teal card tint) so the product reads as the calm answer; frame 5 returns to deep navy with the white logo and a teal pill. The navy-to-light switch at frame 3 is the story turn.
- type: frame.md display (Space Grotesk) for headlines and file names in a mono-feel weight; body (Inter) for small labels. Reference by role.
- motion grammar + reveal model: long-tail power3 eases, smooth over bouncy. Every piece reveals on its spoken cue; nothing front-loads. Holds stay still (subtle jitter at most).
- rhythm: frame 1 builds row by row; frame 2 is the one fast, compressed beat (jostle + slam); frame 3 is a slow push (the relief); frame 4 lets the real recording play; frame 5 is the held end card.
- negative list: no stock shapes, no purple/blue AI gradients, no bokeh, no browser chrome, no emojis, no em dashes. No slideshow (front-load then freeze), no screensaver (everything floating independently).
- caption band: bottom ~17% kept clear in every frame.
